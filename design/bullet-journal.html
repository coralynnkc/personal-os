what this is

The app's layout, unchanged — the same three dashboard regions, the same kanban, the same pipeline, the same week tab. What moved is the surface, and it moved by subtraction: no card fills, no radii, no glass, no ten-hue tag palette. Regions are separated by a single hairline, the way columns in a printed page are.

Each region is treated differently on purpose. The log is plain rows on the page ground. The schedule hangs off a vertical time rail with the now-mark cut into it. Habits are the only filled surface in the app, which is what makes the tracker read as a distinct instrument rather than a third list.

Colour is doing one job: champagne marks what is current — today, now, an active level, the accent under the tab you are on. Coral and amber are reserved for late and due. Nothing else is coloured, so a coloured row always means time.
